# S18: phishing and exfiltration of children's data

Scenario modelled on the Kido nursery case: a staff PC in a kindergarten is
compromised through phishing and tries to send children's records out.
Lab only, invented data, techniques described by category (MITRE ATT&CK
T1566 phishing, T1567 exfiltration over web service, T1071.004 DNS), no
attack code.

| Step | Baseline (no Custos) | Hardened (Custos) | Custos layer | GDPR / CRA |
|------|----------------------|-------------------|--------------|------------|
| 1 Phishing domain | page opens | DNS answer 0.0.0.0 (HaGeZi TIF/pro) | A3 | Art. 32 (security of processing), Art. 25 |
| 2 Guest/child VLAN → staff PC | reachable | dropped (inter-VLAN default drop) | A2 | Art. 5(1)(f) integrity/confidentiality |
| 3 DNS tunnel to the attacker | passes (any resolver) | every query goes to AdGuard Home; DoT/DoH closed; long random names visible per device in the query log | A3 (A12 Suricata later) | Art. 32 |
| 4 Large upload to a new domain | nothing | `upload` alert in the UI overview after 200 MB in 10 min (threshold in custos.json) | A9 | Art. 33 (detect to notify) |
| 5 Event log | none | CSV export with time, kind, device, MAC/IP | A9/A5 | Art. 33 (72 h notification to AZOP), Art. 34 |

**What Custos does NOT prevent:** a login to a cloud service with a stolen
password and no MFA. The traffic is ordinary HTTPS to a legitimate site.
Countermeasure: MFA on every cloud account; Custos can only shorten the
time to detection (step 4).

**Exposure estimate (illustrative, not a promise):** for a kindergarten
of ~100 children, a breach of children's records triggers notification to
AZOP within 72 h and possibly to parents; costs are dominated by incident
handling and reputation, not by fines. Any figure must state its
assumptions; see the plan's offer section. UNVERIFIED.

Run: `tests/demo/S18-verify.sh` from the staff PC (lan1), with
`TOKEN=<login token>` for steps 4-5.
